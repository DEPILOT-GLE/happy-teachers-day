Women who taught the world 👑

Swipe → Educators and leaders who made classrooms into movements. You walk in their footsteps.

🌳

Wangari Maathai

A Kenyan scientist and university lecturer, and the first woman in East and Central Africa to earn a doctorate. In 2004 she became the first African woman to win the Nobel Peace Prize.

🏫

Mary McLeod Bethune

A teacher who opened a school for girls in 1904 with very little money. It grew into a university, and she became an adviser to a US president.

🧒

Maria Montessori

One of Italy's first women doctors, she turned to teaching and created a method now used in schools around the world.

🏛️

Ellen Johnson Sirleaf

Became Africa's first elected woman head of state, as Liberia's president, and won the Nobel Peace Prize in 2011.

🚀

Katherine Johnson

A mathematician who started as a teacher, and whose calculations helped send astronauts to space.
